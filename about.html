About Hult Prize @ UC Davis

A student-led chapter of the Hult Prize — the global student entrepreneurship competition that challenges teams to build for-profit ventures taking on real social and environmental problems.

How it started

Hult Prize @ UC Davis began with one student. As founder and campus director, they built the chapter from the ground up — recruiting its first members, assembling an executive board, and earning faculty support on the way to official university organizational recognition.

From there, the chapter took on the work of running UC Davis's own official Hult Prize OnCampus competition: recruiting judges from both academia and industry, and managing everything from event logistics and communications to judging and team coordination.

More than 20 teams have since participated through the chapter and its broader course ecosystem, with two advancing to the Hult Prize National Competition — see the full numbers on our Impact section →.

Mission & Values

Our mission is to bring the Hult Prize competition — and its social entrepreneurship mission — to UC Davis: giving students the opportunity to develop and pitch impact-driven ventures, and connecting them to the broader global Hult Prize ecosystem.

  • Entrepreneurship

    Turning ideas into real, for-profit ventures.

  • Innovation

    Encouraging bold, creative approaches to hard problems.

  • Social Impact

    Every venture is judged by the real-world problem it solves.

  • Global Perspective

    Connecting UC Davis students to a worldwide competition and network.

  • Student Leadership

    Run by students, for students — from the executive board on down.

Partnership

The ENG 108 Collaboration

One of the chapter's most significant achievements has been its long-term collaboration with ENG 108 at UC Davis. That partnership has been used to expose far more students to entrepreneurship and startup development than the chapter could reach on its own — substantially expanding participation in the competition each year.

Built to Last

When the chapter's founder transferred away from UC Davis, Hult Prize @ UC Davis kept going. The organization continued operating under its executive board — evidence that what was built here was designed to be sustainable, run by a team rather than dependent on any one person.
