Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Hult Prize, our chapter, and how to get involved. A couple of answers below are still being finalized by the chapter — those are marked clearly.

Questions

  • The Hult Prize is a global student entrepreneurship competition. Teams of students design for-profit ventures that address major social and environmental challenges, then pitch and compete through a series of stages toward national and global competition.

  • No — no business background is required to participate.

  • Judges are recruited from academia and industry to evaluate teams at the chapter's OnCampus competition.

  • You can join a team, apply for the executive board, or attend the OnCampus competition — see Get Involved → for details.

  • Check the Event Timeline → page for key dates, and the Blog & Newsletter → page for announcements and recaps.

  • Teams compete at the chapter's OnCampus level first, with top teams advancing toward the Hult Prize National Competition. Specific dates, prize amounts, and eligibility rules change from year to year, so check the official Hult Prize website → for this year's current terms rather than relying on this page for them.
