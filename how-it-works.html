How the Hult Prize Works

From your campus's own OnCampus competition to a $1,000,000 USD pitch in London — the real path a team follows, how judges actually score it, and the causes a venture can take on.

Overview

The Hult Prize is the world's largest student startup competition — a year-long journey that takes a team from a single idea to a shot at $1,000,000 USD in seed funding. Every venture has to be for-profit and has to support at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal. This year's theme, "Unlimited," means teams can build toward any SDG they choose, rather than a single assigned theme.

Below is the real path a team follows from our own OnCampus competition all the way to the Global Finals, how judges actually score teams at each stage, and the Sustainable Development Goals a venture can be built around.

Heads up: competition stages, eligibility, and prize details can change year to year. Always verify the current terms against the official Hult Prize website (hultprize.org ↗) before relying on anything here.
