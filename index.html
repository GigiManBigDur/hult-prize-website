What is the Hult Prize?

The Hult Prize is a global student entrepreneurship competition. Teams of students design for-profit ventures that address major social and environmental challenges, then pitch their ideas and compete through a series of stages toward national and global competition. It turns classroom ideas into real ventures — judged by real entrepreneurs, academics, and industry professionals.

Our story, in brief

Hult Prize @ UC Davis started as one student's idea and became UC Davis's first official Hult Prize chapter — built by recruiting an executive board, earning faculty support, and securing official university recognition. Through a lasting partnership with ENG 108, the chapter kept growing even after its founder transferred away, proving it was built to last, not to depend on one person.
