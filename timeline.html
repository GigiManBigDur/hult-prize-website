Chapter Timeline

The key dates in this cycle's Hult Prize @ UC Davis competition — from kickoff through the National Competition.

Dates shown are placeholders for this page's design — real confirmed dates will replace them before launch.

Event timeline

You Are Here —
  1. Next Up

    September 1, 2026

    Kickoff / Info Session

    6:00 PM [Placeholder Room], Hutchison Hall

    An open info session introducing the Hult Prize @ UC Davis chapter, this cycle's challenge theme, and how team formation and judging will work. Free food, no commitment required — just show up to learn what the season looks like.

  2. Next Up

    September 20, 2026

    Team Formation Deadline

    11:59 PM Online — team roster form

    Deadline for participants to lock in a team roster of 2–5 members for this cycle. Haven't found a team yet? Solo participants who submit the interest form by this date will be matched with others looking for teammates.

  3. Next Up

    October 10, 2026

    Ideation Workshop (ENG 108 partnership)

    5:30 PM [Placeholder Room], ENG 108 building

    A hands-on ideation workshop run in partnership with ENG 108, walking teams through structured brainstorming and problem-framing techniques for early-stage social impact ventures. Come with an open mind, even if you don't have an idea yet.

  4. Next Up

    November 5, 2026

    Venture Development Workshops

    5:30 PM [Placeholder Room], UC Davis

    A series of workshops covering venture fundamentals — market sizing, customer discovery, and a lean business model canvas — to help teams sharpen their concept ahead of the pitch submission deadline.

  5. Next Up

    January 15, 2027

    Pitch Submission Deadline

    11:59 PM Online — pitch deck submission portal

    Final deadline for teams to submit their written pitch deck and supporting materials for review ahead of the OnCampus Competition. Late submissions cannot be scored, so build in buffer time before the cutoff.

  6. Next Up

    February 20, 2027

    OnCampus Competition Day

    12:00 PM [Placeholder Venue], UC Davis

    The chapter's flagship pitch competition, where teams present live in front of a judging panel for a chance to advance to the Hult Prize Regional round. Open to the campus community to attend and watch.

  7. Next Up

    March 1, 2027

    Results Announcement

    7:00 PM [Placeholder Room], UC Davis

    Winners of the OnCampus Competition are announced, along with which team(s) will advance to represent UC Davis at the next stage of the Hult Prize competition.

  8. Next Up

    April 15, 2027

    National Competition (for advancing teams)

    10:00 AM [Placeholder City / Venue] (set by the Hult Prize Foundation)

    The advancing team(s) compete against other university chapters nationally, with top performers moving on toward the global Hult Prize accelerator and summit.
